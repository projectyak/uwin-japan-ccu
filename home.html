Why Now

Three forces are reshaping which suppliers
Japan's tier-1 buyers retain — and which they replace.

Decarbonization has moved from "a goal" to "a condition of trade." Suppliers that cannot produce verified emissions data are being deselected — quietly, and quickly.

01
Scope 3 disclosure is now a procurement requirement
Tier-1 buyers in Japan now require verified CO₂ data from their suppliers. Those that cannot supply it are removed from preferred-supplier lists at the next renewal cycle.
02
GX subsidies expand, but the application bar rises
METI's GX-aligned subsidies grow year on year, while documentation requirements grow with them. Most mid-tier manufacturers lack the internal capacity to complete a compliant submission.
03
Product Carbon Footprint shifts from bonus to baseline
An ISO 14067-aligned per-product CO₂ figure is no longer a differentiator. It is increasingly a precondition for inclusion in RFPs.
CCU·XPO — UWin's answer

One integrated system replaces
three separate vendor engagements.

CCU·XPO combines two layers into one offering: the Airwin capture equipment, with commercial operating experience, and a compliance and subsidy platform engineered for the Japanese regulatory environment. Airwin is proven; the platform is new — together, they replace the equipment vendor, the consultant, and the MRV tool a supplier would otherwise contract separately.

Hardware · Proven
CCU capture equipment
Airwin is UWin·Japan's skid-mounted CCU equipment. It captures CO₂ from factory exhaust and converts it into sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃) and sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃) — valuable chemicals with established downstream markets. Airwin is in commercial operation in Indonesia today.
Platform · Launching
Compliance & subsidy layer
Six IoT sensors stream operating data to a single source-of-truth. METI / GX subsidy packages and ISO 14067 PCF reports auto-generate from the same data — no duplicate entry. Launching for Japan now; early adopters help shape the operational profile.

Environmental impact. Economic return.

Environmental value

Counts as Scope 1 reduction, not just offset
